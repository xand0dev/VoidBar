Your MacBook notch, finally useful.

VoidBar turns the dead space around the notch into a panel that opens on hover: music, files, clipboard, notes, translation, focus, and your AI coding limits — then gets out of the way.

Overview

Everything you use, at a glance.

The panel opens onto an Overview built from the tabs you actually keep. No weather if you never check it — just what matters to you.

  • The player takes the big card while something plays; the rest fill a grid.
  • Copy in one click — the latest clipboard entries and your snippets.
  • Focus timer, latest note, shelf, AI limits — each opens its full tab.
  • Your order — switch widgets on and off in Preferences.
Live island

The notch comes alive.

While music plays or a focus session runs, the notch grows a wing on each side — the cover or a progress ring on the left, a live equalizer or countdown on the right. Hover, and it pours open.

Inside

Thirteen small tools. One hover away.

Every tab can be hidden or reordered. Pick one to see it.

For AI coders

Claude Code and Codex limits, in the notch.

See how much of your five-hour and weekly windows is used, and when each resets. Codex is read from its local logs; Claude from your account, only when you press refresh.

  • Codex works out of the box from its own session logs.
  • Claude: press ↻ for the exact numbers /usage shows — never in the background.
  • Amber and red only when a limit gets tight.
Make it yours

Your colours. Your aurora.

Pick an accent — six presets or any colour — and let the aurora follow what is on screen, your accent, or turn it off. The panel itself stays black, so it always melts into the notch.

Try it: this page follows your pick too.
Privacy

No account. No analytics. No server.

VoidBar is native SwiftUI and AppKit — no Electron. Everything stays on your Mac unless a feature you opened says otherwise.

Clipboard stays in memory

History lives only while VoidBar runs. Password managers' entries are ignored.

Every request is documented

Weather, TickTick, and Spotify artwork are VoidBar's only network calls — each listed in PRIVACY.md.

Translation on device

English ↔ Ukrainian through Apple's Translation framework, not a cloud service.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

macOS 15 Sequoia or later on an Apple Silicon Mac.

  1. Download the disk imageGet the latest VoidBar-…-arm64.dmg from GitHub Releases.
  2. Drag VoidBar to ApplicationsThen open it. Its icon appears in the menu bar and the panel opens when you hover over the notch.
  3. Allow the first launchThe build is not notarized yet, so macOS warns once. Choose Done (not Move to Trash), open System Settings → Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.

Optional: verify the download against the checksum published with the release.

shasum -a 256 -c VoidBar-*-arm64.dmg.sha256

Prefer to build it yourself?

git clone https://github.com/xand0dev/VoidBar.git && cd VoidBar && ./Scripts/bundle.sh && open build/VoidBar.app
